About The Brass Bulldog

The Brass Bulldog Sushi House blends traditional sushi craftsmanship with the imagination, machinery, and character of a steam-powered world.

Our Story

The Brass Bulldog Sushi House was created around one idea: bring together quality food, memorable design, and a little imagination.

Our steampunk-inspired atmosphere gives the restaurant its personality, but the focus stays on the food. The menu is built around approachable sushi, bowls, and specialty dishes that are easy to enjoy whether you are a longtime sushi fan or trying something new.

What We Do

We aim to make every visit simple, welcoming, and memorable.

Our menu offers options throughout the day, from lighter breakfast selections to larger lunch and dinner meals, with each dish prepared to balance flavor, presentation, and variety.

More Than a Theme

The gears, brass, machinery, and airship-inspired details are part of what makes The Brass Bulldog unique, but they are only one part of the experience.

At its core, the restaurant is meant to be a comfortable place to share a meal, try something different, and enjoy good food with good company.
